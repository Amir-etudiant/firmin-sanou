Frequently asked

All you need to know before buying

How does shipping work?

Each sculpture is hand-packed by Firmin in a reinforced wooden crate. Insured tracked shipping worldwide. Allow 2 to 4 weeks depending on destination (Europe is faster). Shipping is included in the displayed price for mainland France and Europe.

Is payment secure?

Payment is processed by Stripe, the world leader in online payments. Credit card, Apple Pay, Google Pay accepted. No card data is stored on the site. Installment payments available for pieces over €1,000.

Is there a certificate of authenticity?

Yes. Each sculpture comes with a certificate hand-signed by Firmin Sanou, listing the title, year, dimensions, material and a serial number. Your guarantee that the piece is unique.

What if I don't like the sculpture?

You have 14 days from receipt to change your mind. Return the piece in its original packaging for a full refund (excluding return shipping).

VAT and customs?

For France and the European Union, the displayed price is all included. For shipments outside the EU (USA, Canada, Switzerland, etc.), local customs fees may apply on receipt. We provide all necessary invoices for clearance.
